# inertia snap

Flick physics in one dimension. Drag or scroll, release, it glides and lands.

Direct follow while dragging, friction on release, a critically damped spring takes over the remaining velocity. No easing curves, no keyframes, just integration.

Vanilla JS, ~40 lines of physics. Works for carousels, pickers, timelines.

## use

Open `demo.html`. `P` holds friction, spring, follow, edge behavior. `STEP` is the item pitch.
